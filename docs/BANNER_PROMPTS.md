## 🎨 Option 1: "The Oracle of Knowledge" (HERO BANNER)

**Style**: Epic mythological meets futuristic  
**Use for**: Website hero, main GitHub banner

```
A majestic 16:9 banner featuring Apollo, the Greek god of knowledge and wisdom, 
seated upon a throne of golden light at the center. His form radiates warm 
golden sunlight, with rays extending outward like the canopy of an enormous 
tree. Instead of leaves, the tree's branches are made of glowing interconnected 
nodes and neural pathways forming a vast knowledge graph - each node a star 
or data point connected by luminous golden threads. The roots of the tree 
extend downward into a sea of flowing code, books, and data streams 
representing sources of knowledge. Apollo holds a lyre in one hand, its 
strings forming constellation patterns. Around him float holographic displays 
showing equations and neural networks. The sky is a blend of ancient Greek 
cosmos imagery with subtle circuit board patterns. Color palette: deep royal 
blue (#0A1628) and midnight purple (#1A0A2E) background, radiant gold (#FFD700), 
warm amber (#FF9500), soft white (#FFF8E7), and electric cyan (#00E5FF) for 
tech accents. Epic cinematic composition, dramatic volumetric lighting, 
hyper-detailed, 8k resolution, concept art by ARTGOD and Roger Dean meets 
digital art.
```
